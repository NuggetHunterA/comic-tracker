# Surface brief: Comic-Tracker app (all screens)

Mode: Operate. Platform web (installable PWA), primary device Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8: cover screen (one column) and unfolded inner screen (two panes).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal reading planner that looks like the flyer wall outside a comic shop: tonight's issues are taped-up flyers. Refuses the category default of grey cards with a blue accent.

OWN-WORLD: Photocopy paper grey #e9e8e2, toner black #111, mid grey #8d8c86, one fluoro pink spot #ff2e88 for state (active tab, unlock dates, followed marks). Anton for headings, Archivo Narrow for UI text, Courier Prime only for dates and counts. Masking-tape strips, halftone dots on placeholder covers, slight rotation (max 2 degrees) on tonight's covers only.

STORY: Adrian opens the app in the evening, sees tonight's three issues, reads, ticks them off with a 1 to 10 rating, and glances at what unlocks on Infinite next.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Plan screen. Date stamp in pink, "HEUTE ABEND" in Anton, three taped covers of one story arc, Gelesen buttons under them, next evenings as a dated list, the next Infinite unlock as a black strip with a pink date sticker, black bottom nav (Plan, Stapel, Katalog, Serien). Unfolded: plan on the left, selected issue detail on the right.

FORM: Torn Flyer / Photocopy Wall (user-chosen, interpreted from the name; Impeccable catalog unreachable). Signature move: an issue that is not yet readable is shown as a grayscale photocopy with a pink date sticker; it turns into its full-colour cover when it unlocks. seed: f26c0dd1b89c2d9b

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
